Left panel — Configuration: setting of all the options before launching. Fixed width (390 px). Settings are restored automatically at each start. The equivalent CLI command is displayed under the ▶ Generate the SBOM button and is updated in real time.

Right panel — Results: fourteen tabs — Progress (with log export and summary), Results (files + sbomqs score), Dashboard (multi-scanner CVE synthesis), Grype, OSV-Scanner and Trivy (CVE analyses), CRA Compliance (Cyber Resilience Act compliance report), SBOM Quality (sbomqs
sbom-scorecard), SBOM Tree (exploration of the SBOM components), SBOM Comparison (diff of two SBOMs), SBOM Merge (combines several SBOMs into one), SBOM Licenses (visualization of the SBOM licenses and compliance report in AsciiDoc, Markdown, HTML, CSV or JSON), SBOM Viewer (interactive exploration of an external SBOM file) and Preview (preview of the SBOM content).

The ❓ button in the title bar opens this user manual directly in the application (table of contents on the left, selected chapter on the right) — not to be confused with the smaller ❓ icons present on each field/section, which display a short tooltip on hover instead of opening the full manual. The search field at the top of the table of contents filters the chapters by title and by content as you type; an excerpt of the passage found is displayed under the title of the matching chapter.

The 🌙 / ☀ button in the title bar switches between dark and light mode. The 🎨 button opens the theme color picker. Both preferences are saved.

The 文A button (interface language) offers System, Français and English. System follows the language of the workstation, falling back to French if it is not supported. The choice applies immediately and is saved. The language is also forwarded to the sbom-generator CLI launched by the GUI (via SBOM_LANG), so the messages of the Progress tab follow it.

Note
 The interface, the exported reports (AsciiDoc/PDF), the CLI messages and this manual are available in French and English. Machine-read contents (SBOM files, Error: / Warning: prefixes, JSON keys) are never translated.
