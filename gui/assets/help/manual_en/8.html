9. Tasks tab — Scan queue

The Tasks tab queues vulnerability scans: they run one at a time (scanners are heavy and share their vulnerability database) and their results feed the Dashboard (cards, comparison, remediation, trend, report).

  1. Choose the SBOM to scan: the first generated JSON SBOM is proposed automatically, Browse… picks another one.

  2. Tick the scanners (Grype, OSV-Scanner, Trivy).

  3. Add to queue: one scan per ticked scanner appears.

Each row shows its state — Queued, Running… (with the duration), Done (number of vulnerabilities), Failed (error message: tool not found, unreadable output…) or Cancelled — and offers:

  • Cancel (⏹): removes a queued scan, or stops the process of a running one;

  • Retry (↺): puts a failed or cancelled scan back in the queue;

  • Remove (✕): deletes the row.

Cancel all interrupts every queued or running scan; Remove finished cleans the list. CISA KEV / EPSS / PoC enrichment follows the "enrich online" setting of the scan tabs.

Note
 The results of a queued scan replace those of the same scanner in the dashboard; the Grype / OSV-Scanner / Trivy tab tables do not show them (they keep their own scans).
